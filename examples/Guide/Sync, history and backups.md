## Sync and offline

Notes save as you type and appear on your other devices right away. Choose how in **Settings → Sync**:

| Mode | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| Live | Saves as you type; other devices' changes arrive at once |
| Interval | Saves as you type; checks other devices on a timer |
| Manual | Edits stay on this device until you press Sync (`Ctrl S`) |

When the connection drops, you can keep reading, editing, and even creating notes. Everything is sent when the server is back. If a note changed on another device in the meantime, the status bar says **Conflict**: click it to keep your version, theirs, or both.

## Version history

Earlier versions of each note are kept as you edit, at most every 10 minutes by default. `Alt H` opens them. **Changes** shows what's different, with the edited words marked, and any version can be restored.

## Trash and archive

- **Deleted notes** go to the trash: the bin in the left rail. Click one to read it, then restore it or delete it for good. The trash empties itself after 30 days.
- **Archived notes** are put away without being deleted: right-click → **Archive**. They leave the note list, search, and links, and open read-only from the box in the left rail until you unarchive them.

## Backups

- **Settings → Sync → Download .zip** gives you every note, folder, and attachment.
- **Scheduled backups** write a zip on the server every hour, day, or week, keeping as many as you like.
- **Git**, if you like, commits the notes on a schedule and can push them to a remote.

## Sharing and exporting

- Right-click a note → **Share link** for a read-only link, optionally one that expires.
- The note menu → **Export** makes a PDF, a self-contained HTML file, or plain Markdown.
- **Settings → Files and links → Import** brings in `.md` files, images, or a `.zip` of a folder or an Obsidian vault.

## Accounts

Each account is its own set of notes, with its own password (optional), tags, history, trash, and settings. Add one from the account picture at the bottom of the left rail.
