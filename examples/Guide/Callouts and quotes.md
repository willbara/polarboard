## Quotes

> The secret of getting ahead is getting started.
> — Mark Twain

A line that starts with `>` is a quote. Several in a row make one block.

## Callouts

A quote that starts with `[!kind]` becomes a callout. Anything after the kind is its title.

> [!note]
> A plain note. Without a title, the kind is the title.

> [!tip] Keep notes small
> One idea per note, and links between them, is easier to find later than one long document.

> [!warning] Before you upgrade
> Download a backup from **Settings → Sync** first. It takes a second.

> [!danger] Deleting for good
> Notes deleted from the trash can't be brought back.

> [!success] Saved
> Callouts can hold **formatting**, [[Links and embeds|links]] and lists:
> - like this
> - and this

The `/callout` command in the slash menu inserts one for you.
