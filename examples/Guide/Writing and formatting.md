Polarboard is a Markdown editor. It colors the Markdown as you type, shows it rendered in **Read** mode (`Ctrl E`), and puts both side by side in **Split** mode (the buttons at the top right).

## Emphasis

**Bold**, *italic*, ***both at once***, ~~struck through~~, ==highlighted== and `inline code`.

Each of these has its own color, a shade of your accent color by default. Pick your own, or turn the colors off, in **Settings → Appearance → Text colors**.

`Ctrl B` makes the selection bold, `Ctrl I` italic, and `Ctrl K` turns it into a link. The toolbar above the note does the same with the mouse.

Headings 1 and 2 work the same way, with one or two `#`. Every heading shows up in the **Outline** panel of the sidebar, where a click jumps to it.

## The slash menu

Type `/` at the start of a line for a menu of things to insert: headings, checklists, tables, callouts, code, math, diagrams, embeds, templates, and properties. Keep typing to narrow it down, like `/table`.

## Writing checks

[Harper](https://github.com/Automattic/harper) checks spelling, grammar, and style while you write, entirely in your browser. This sentance has a typo on purpose: right-click the underline to fix it, add the word to your dictionary, ignore it, or turn that kind of check off. `F8` jumps to the next suggestion.

## Edit in place

In **Read** mode, click any paragraph, list, or table to edit its Markdown right there. Click below the last block to add a new one. You can turn this off in Settings.

## Find and replace

`Ctrl F` finds in the open note, and `Ctrl H` also replaces. Match case, whole words and regular expressions are buttons in the bar, and **Replace all** is one step to undo.

## Focus

`Ctrl Shift F` hides everything but the page. Point at the top or bottom edge to bring the bars back, or press `Esc`.
