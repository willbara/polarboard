Polarboard keeps what you write as plain **Markdown files** on your own server. Nothing is locked in: they're ordinary files and folders, so you can open them in any editor, sync them, or back them up however you like. #polarboard

> [!tip] These notes are a tour
> Each one shows a feature by using it. Read them in **Read** mode (`Ctrl E`), then press it again to see the Markdown behind them. When you're done, delete them: they go to the trash, and you can bring them back from the bin in the left rail.

## Examples from everyday use

- **Projects** is a folder with its own page: click the folder in the sidebar to open [[Projects]]. Inside are [[Garden planner]], [[Trip to Lisbon]] and [[Reading list]].
- **Daily** holds two daily notes. `Alt D` opens today's, made from a template.
- **Templates** holds [[Daily note]] and [[Meeting notes]]. Insert one with `Alt Shift T` or by typing `/template`.
- The **archive** (the box in the left rail) holds an old project, read-only and out of the way.
- [[Frases em português]] turns writing checks off with a single property.
